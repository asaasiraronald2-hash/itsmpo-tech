Who we are

Building technology around real life.

itsmpo is a technology company focused on developing digital products for everyday needs. Our product ecosystem spans commerce, business tools, veterinary services, delivery, transport and family connections.

We believe technology should be useful, accessible and thoughtfully designed. Our goal is to turn real challenges into digital experiences that people and businesses can use with confidence.

Starting from Uganda, we aim to create solutions with the potential to serve communities across Africa and beyond.
